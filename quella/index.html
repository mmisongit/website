Getting started

  1. Open Quella and tap SOS. The breathing guide starts right away, with no sign-up and no paywall.
  2. Follow the dot along the wave: breathe in as it rises, out as it falls. With the vibration guide on, you can close your eyes and feel each breath.
  3. For quicker access, add the Quella widget, the Control Center button or the Action Button, or say “Quella SOS” to Siri.

Quella helps you breathe through hard moments. It does not replace therapy, medical care or emergency services. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. Quella lists crisis lines under “Get help now”.

Purchases

Quella Pro

SOS breathing, three patterns, the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise, crisis lines and all shortcuts are free. Quella Pro adds all patterns, your own patterns, guided programs, the full history, a daily reminder and Mindful Minutes in Apple Health. It is available as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, or as a monthly subscription. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Quella can remind you two days before your trial ends, if you allow it after starting the trial.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Quella → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel, open your Apple Account settings → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Data & privacy

Quella has no account. Your sessions, patterns and progress stay on your iPhone. To move to a new iPhone, create a backup under Settings → Your Data, keep the file in the Files app and restore it on the new device.

With Quella Pro you can save finished sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. Quella only writes this one data type and reads nothing from Health. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. Read the privacy policy and Apple standard terms of use.

Help & troubleshooting

I don’t feel the vibration

  • In Quella, open Settings and turn on “Vibration guide”.
  • In the iOS Settings app, open Sounds & Haptics and turn on System Haptics.
  • Keep Quella open: iOS pauses vibration while an app is in the background. The Live Activity keeps counting so you can return to the same breath.

Mindful Minutes don’t appear in Health

  • Turn on “Save to Apple Health” in Quella → Settings (Quella Pro).
  • In the Health app, open Sharing → Apps → Quella and allow Mindful Minutes.

Contact support

Need help? Email support@mmcapitals.com with your iOS version, Quella version and a description of the issue. Quella support cannot provide medical or crisis help.
