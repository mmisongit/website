Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Quella is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your practice stays on your iPhone

Sessions, your own breathing patterns, program progress, history and settings are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Quella has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours. The breathing guide, grounding exercise and crisis lines work offline.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Quella’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

Apple Health

Saving to Apple Health is optional and part of Quella Pro. If you turn it on, Quella asks iOS for permission to write one data type: Mindful Minutes. Each finished session is saved with its start and end time. Quella does not read any data from Apple Health.

Quella does not send Apple Health data to RevenueCat or to any server, and does not use it for advertising. Mindful Minutes saved by Quella are then handled by your Health settings, including iCloud sync for Health and the access you give other apps. You can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app; values already saved stay in Health until you delete them there.

Reminders

The optional daily reminder and the reminder before a free trial ends are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off at any time in Quella or in the iOS Settings app.

Backups you create

You can export a backup file of your Quella data. It is created only when you request it, and you decide where it is saved or shared.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Quella Pro. Quella uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Quella Pro entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. No name, email, payment-card details, session data or health data are supplied by Quella to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained as needed to provide and restore your subscription and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat may process data outside your country under its published data-processing terms.

No analytics, no advertising

Quella contains no analytics SDK, no tracking and no in-app advertising. We do not sell your data.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. You can delete all of Quella’s local data by deleting the App. You can request access to or deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat by contacting support; an anonymous RevenueCat identifier or App Store order reference may be needed to locate them. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction, restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Not medical advice

Quella is a wellness tool for guided breathing. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace therapy, medical care or emergency services.

Children

Quella is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This page is updated when Quella’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
