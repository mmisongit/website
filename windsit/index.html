Getting started

  1. Add your first stand: tap “Use my location” while you stand at it, or pick it on the map.
  2. Tap the winds the stand can be hunted on, the directions the wind may come from without carrying your scent to where the deer come in.
  3. Open Today before you head out: the compass shows the wind and where your scent drifts, the chip tells you whether to sit, and the day rating lists its reasons.

Shooting light is shown as a guide (30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset). Shooting hours vary by state, species and season. Always check your state’s regulations.

Purchases

Windsit Pro

Today and tomorrow, one stand, the current and measured wind are free. Windsit Pro adds unlimited stands, the 10-day outlook with ratings and the solunar calendar, the evening heads-up and widgets. It is available as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase with Family Sharing. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore or cancel

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Windsit → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account settings → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Data & privacy

Windsit has no account. Your stands stay on your iPhone. To get weather, Windsit sends a stand’s position, rounded to about 2 km, to Apple Weather and to the National Weather Service. To move to a new iPhone, create a backup under Settings → Your Data and restore it on the new device.

Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. Read the privacy policy and Apple standard terms of use.

Help & troubleshooting

“Use my location” does not work

  • Allow location access for Windsit in the iOS Settings app (While Using the App).
  • Or tap the map to place the stand instead.

No measured wind

  • The measured wind comes from National Weather Service stations and is available in the United States only. The forecast works everywhere.

The forecast looks old

  • Pull down on Today to refresh. Without a connection Windsit shows the last forecast and says how old it is.

Contact support

Need help? Email support@mmcapitals.com with your iOS version, Windsit version and a description of the issue.
