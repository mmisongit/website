Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Windsit is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your stands stay on your iPhone

Stands (name, position, time zone, good winds and notes) and settings are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Windsit has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Windsit’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

Location

Windsit asks for location access only when you tap “Use my location” to place a stand, and only while you use the App. The position is saved with that stand. Windsit does not track your location in the background.

Weather requests

To show weather, Windsit sends a stand’s position, rounded to about 2 km, to Apple Weather (Apple WeatherKit) for forecasts and to the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for the latest measurement of the nearest weather station. These requests contain no name, account or device identifier of ours. See Apple’s privacy policy and the National Weather Service privacy policy. Sun, moon and solunar times are calculated on your iPhone.

Windsit keeps the latest forecasts on your device so it works offline. Widgets read only this saved data.

Notifications

The optional evening heads-up is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone from saved forecasts. You can turn it off at any time in Windsit or in the iOS Settings app.

Backups you create

You can export a backup file of your stands. It is created only when you request it, and you decide where it is saved or shared.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Windsit Pro. Windsit uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Windsit Pro entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. No name, email, payment-card details, locations or stands are supplied by Windsit to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained as needed to provide and restore your purchase and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat may process data outside your country under its published data-processing terms.

No analytics, no advertising

Windsit contains no analytics SDK, no tracking and no in-app advertising. We do not sell your data.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. You can delete all of Windsit’s local data by deleting the App. You can request access to or deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat by contacting support; an anonymous RevenueCat identifier or App Store order reference may be needed to locate them. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction, restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Shooting hours

Shooting light in Windsit is a guide. Hunting regulations, including legal shooting hours, are set by each state. Always check the rules that apply to you.

Children

Windsit is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This page is updated when Windsit’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
