Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Nephwise is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your diary stays on your iPhone

Your daily limits, the foods and drinks you log, your own foods, saved meals, report details and settings are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Nephwise has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours, and does not sync your diary to iCloud. Food search and the US barcode catalog work offline.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Nephwise’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

Barcode lookups with Open Food Facts

When you scan a barcode that is not in the food database inside the App, Nephwise asks Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) for that product. Only the barcode number is sent, together with the App’s name; nothing about you or your diary. Open Food Facts receives your IP address as part of the connection, under its privacy policy. Results are kept on your iPhone for up to 30 days so the same product does not need to be looked up again.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to photograph nutrition labels. A label photo, or a photo you choose from your library, is read on your iPhone with Apple’s text recognition and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Apple Health

Writing to Apple Health is optional, off by default and part of Nephwise Pro. If you turn it on, Nephwise asks iOS for permission to write dietary potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein and water. For each new entry, Nephwise saves the known values with the time of the entry; values that are unknown are not written. Deleting or changing an entry removes or replaces its values in Health. Nephwise does not read any data from Apple Health.

Nephwise does not send Apple Health data to RevenueCat or to any server and does not use it for advertising. Values saved by Nephwise are then handled by your Health settings, including iCloud sync for Health and the access you give other apps. You can withdraw permission at any time in the Health app.

Reports and backups you create

The PDF report, the CSV file and the backup file are created only when you ask for them, and you decide where they are saved or shared through the iOS share sheet. Nephwise does not send them anywhere itself.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Nephwise Pro. Nephwise uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Nephwise Pro entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. No name, email, payment-card details, diary data or health data are supplied by Nephwise to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained as needed to provide and restore your purchase and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat may process data outside your country under its published data-processing terms.

No analytics, no advertising

Nephwise contains no analytics SDK, no tracking and no in-app advertising. We do not sell your data.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. You can delete all of Nephwise’s local data by deleting the App. You can request access to or deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat by contacting support; an anonymous RevenueCat identifier or App Store order reference may be needed to locate them. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction, restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Not medical advice

Nephwise is a food diary. It does not give medical advice, does not set or recommend limits, and is not a medical device. Talk to your nephrologist or renal dietitian before changing your diet.

Children

Nephwise is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This page is updated when Nephwise’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
