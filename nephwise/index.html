Nephwise support

A food diary for chronic kidney disease and dialysis: potassium, phosphorus, sodium, protein and fluid against the limits your care team gave you.

Getting started Purchases Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Open Nephwise and choose your units: mg or mmol, sodium or salt, ml or fl oz.
  2. Enter the daily limits from your nephrologist or renal dietitian. Leave a field empty if that value is not limited for you. You can skip this and add limits later in Settings.
  3. Tap + to log food: search, pick a recent food, scan a barcode or photograph a nutrition label. Use the drink buttons for fluids.
  4. Open Report to share a PDF with your care team.

Nephwise is a food diary. It does not give medical advice and does not set or recommend limits. Talk to your nephrologist or renal dietitian before changing your diet.

Purchases

Nephwise Pro

Logging food and drinks, the daily view, the last 7 days of history and a 7-day PDF report are free. Nephwise Pro adds history and trends for 30 and 90 days, reports for any period up to 90 days, CSV export, saved meals and writing to Apple Health. It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription (the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial) or as a one-time purchase. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore or cancel

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Nephwise → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account settings → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Data & privacy

Nephwise has no account. Your diary stays on your iPhone and is not synced to iCloud. To move to a new iPhone, create a backup under Settings → Your Data, keep the file in the Files app and restore it on the new device.

Food values come from USDA FoodData Central. When a scanned barcode is not in the App, Nephwise looks it up at Open Food Facts and sends only the barcode. Read the privacy policy and Apple standard terms of use.

Help & troubleshooting

A value says “unknown”

  • The food source has no value for that nutrient; packaged foods in the US rarely list phosphorus. Nephwise never counts unknown values as zero, and the daily view shows how many entries have no value.
  • If you know the value, add the product under My foods, or scan its nutrition label.

A barcode is not found

  • Without internet, only US packaged foods can be found. Connect and scan again, or scan the nutrition label instead.
  • What you enter for a barcode is saved, so the next scan finds it.

Values don’t appear in Health

  • Turn on “Write to Apple Health” in Nephwise → Settings (Nephwise Pro).
  • In the Health app, open Sharing → Apps → Nephwise and allow the data types.
  • Only entries logged after turning it on are written.
