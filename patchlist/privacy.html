Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Patchlist is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your projects stay on your device

Projects, areas, items, photos, markup, notes and your company profile (name, contact details, logo) are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Patchlist has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include Patchlist’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

Camera, photos, microphone and speech

Patchlist asks for camera access to photograph items and lets you add photos from your library through the system photo picker, which only gives the App the photos you choose. Dictation uses the microphone and Apple’s speech recognition on your device; audio is not stored and not sent to us. The optional write-up of dictated notes uses Apple Intelligence on your device. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Reports you share

PDF reports and CSV files are created on your device. They leave the App only when you share them through the iOS share sheet, to the recipients and apps you choose.

Usage analytics

To learn which features help and where the App can improve, Patchlist sends anonymous usage events to Amplitude, for example that a project or item was created, a report was exported or shared, or a screen like the upgrade screen was shown, with counts such as the number of items or photos. These events are tied to a random identifier created by the App, not to your name, Apple Account or device identifier. Patchlist does not send project content, photos, notes, client names or company details to Amplitude, and turns off Amplitude’s collection of IP address, city, region, country and carrier. See Amplitude’s privacy notice.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Patchlist Pro. Patchlist uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Patchlist Pro entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. Patchlist also gives RevenueCat the random Amplitude identifier, so that purchase events can be counted together with the usage events above. No name, email, payment-card details or project content are supplied by Patchlist to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase and usage records are retained as needed to provide and restore your purchase, to improve the App and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat and Amplitude may process data outside your country under their published data-processing terms.

No tracking, no advertising

Patchlist does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, does not use the advertising identifier and shows no ads. We do not sell your data.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. You can delete all of Patchlist’s local data by deleting the App. You can request access to or deletion of purchase and usage records by contacting support; a purchase date or App Store order reference helps us locate them. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction, restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Children

Patchlist is a tool for construction and property professionals and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This page is updated when Patchlist’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
