Patchlist support

Punch lists and snag lists, done on site: photos with markup, dictation and clean PDF reports. Patchlist works without an account.

Getting started

  1. Create a project for each site or job, or explore the sample project first.
  2. Tap New Item to capture an item: take photos, mark them up with pen, arrows and text, and add a title, trade, area and due date. Or tap the microphone and say what needs fixing.
  3. Swipe an item to set it in progress or resolved.
  4. Tap Report to create a PDF for your client and share it with any app, or export a CSV for Excel or Numbers.

Add your company name, contact details and logo under Settings → Company Profile; they appear on the report’s cover page.

Purchases

Patchlist Pro

The free plan includes one project with every feature; PDF reports carry a small Patchlist watermark. The sample project doesn’t count. Patchlist Pro adds unlimited projects, reports without watermark and with your logo, and CSV export. It is available as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, as a monthly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Patchlist → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel a subscription, open Patchlist → Settings → Manage Subscription, or your Apple Account settings → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

Data & privacy

Patchlist has no account. Projects, items, photos and your company profile stay on your device. Dictation and the optional Apple Intelligence write-up run on your device. Reports and CSV files leave the app only when you share them yourself.

Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. Patchlist sends anonymous usage events to Amplitude to learn which features help. Read the privacy policy and Apple standard terms of use.

Help & troubleshooting

Dictation doesn’t start

  • Allow microphone and speech recognition for Patchlist in the iOS Settings app.
  • The first time, iOS downloads a speech model for your language. Keep the app open until it finishes; after that, dictation works offline.
  • The write-up by Apple Intelligence needs a device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, your dictated words go into the item as you said them.

My logo isn’t on the report

  • The logo is part of Patchlist Pro. Add it under Settings → Company Profile.

Moving to a new iPhone

  • Patchlist data is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup). Restore that backup on the new iPhone. Sync between devices is planned for a later version.
