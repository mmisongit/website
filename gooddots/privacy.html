Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Good Dots: One Line a Day app, called Punkte in German (the “App”), provided by Mohamed Makled (the “Provider”, “we”), handles information. The Provider is responsible for processing in connection with the App and can be reached at support@mmcapitals.com.

At a glance: Everything you write in Good Dots stays on your iPhone. The App has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours. The Provider does not receive your entries.

Your entries

The sentences you write, the areas and colors you set up, the day of each entry and your reminder time are stored locally on your iPhone. The Provider does not receive, store or have access to this data. The data is included in your device backup under your Apple Account.

Widget

The Home Screen widget reads your entries on your device to show today’s entry and your recent days. Nothing leaves your device for this.

Export

The text export is created on your device. It leaves your device only when you share it yourself, for example by email or by saving it to a file.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service is used. If you answer in the notification, your reply is saved on your device like any other entry.

Purchases and ratings

The App is free and has no in-app purchases. The App may ask whether you want to rate it; ratings and reviews are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple may provide us with aggregated download reports that do not identify you. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account or registration.
  • No analytics of how you use the App and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to answer you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Your entries stay on your device, so you control them directly: you can edit, delete or export them in the App at any time. Deleting the App removes the data it stored on your device. For questions, email support@mmcapitals.com.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The current version and its date are always posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mmcapitals.com.
