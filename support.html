Unroller support

Clean up your camera roll, one swipe at a time. Review and organize your photos on iPhone.

Getting started Purchases Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Open Unroller and allow access to the photos you want to organize.
  2. Review your photos and choose which to keep or delete.
  3. Check your selection before confirming a deletion.

Purchases

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account as for your original purchase and choose Restore purchases in Unroller. If access is still missing, contact support with the app version and your App Store order reference. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Manage a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your iPhone’s Settings, select your name, then Subscriptions to view or change your Unroller subscription.

Data & privacy

Your photo library

Your photos and videos are processed on your device. The privacy policy explains photo access, purchases, optional analytics and advertising measurement.

Before deleting photos

Review your selection carefully before confirming. Deleting items changes your Photos library; keep an independent backup of photos you want to preserve.

Help & troubleshooting

Some photos are missing

Check the photo-library access you granted to Unroller in iPhone Settings. If you selected only some photos, Unroller can only work with the items you allowed.

Contact support

Email support@mmcapitals.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Unroller version and what happened. Please avoid sending private photos or videos.
