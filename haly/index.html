Haly support

A calm journal for your GLP-1 shots, tablets, weight and wellbeing. Your records stay on your iPhone.

Getting started Purchases Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Open Haly and choose to start your own journal, or explore the demo first.
  2. Add your medication with its amount, form and schedule.
  3. Log each dose, your weight and how you feel. You can correct or delete any entry later.

Purchases

Haly Plus

Logging, the full history, one medication, export and backup are free. Haly Plus adds reminders, the weight trend chart and several medications. It is available as a yearly subscription, with a free trial where offered, or as a monthly subscription. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore a subscription

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Haly → Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel, use Settings → Manage subscription or your Apple Account settings.

Data & privacy

Moving to a new iPhone

Haly has no account. Create an encrypted backup under Settings → Data & backup, keep the file and its password, and restore it in Haly on the new iPhone. iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may also include Haly’s data.

Health records stay on your device. Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. Read the privacy policy and terms of use. Haly does not give medical advice.

Help & troubleshooting

Reminders do not arrive

  • Check that Haly Plus is active and that the medication has “Reminder on”.
  • In Haly, open Settings → Reminders & notifications and allow notifications.
  • In the iOS Settings app, check Notifications → Haly and your Focus modes.
  • Open Haly from time to time: iOS allows a limited number of scheduled notifications, and Haly plans further reminders when you open it.

Contact support

Need help? Email support@mmcapitals.com with your iOS version, Haly version and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending health records.
