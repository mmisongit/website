Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Haly is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your health records stay on your iPhone

Medications, doses, injection sites, weight, wellbeing entries and notes are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Haly has no account and no cloud sync. We never receive these records, and they are not sent to RevenueCat, Apple Ads or any other third party.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS may include Haly’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Their text is neutral: the medication name and amount do not appear in the notification. You can turn notifications off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Exports and backups you create

You can export your records as a CSV file or as a password-encrypted backup. These files are created only when you ask for them, and you decide where they are saved or shared. CSV files are not encrypted. We cannot recover a backup password.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Haly Plus. Haly uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Haly Plus entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. No name, email, payment-card details or health records are supplied by Haly to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained as needed to provide and restore your subscription and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat may process data outside your country under its published data-processing terms.

Apple Ads attribution

If you installed Haly after seeing an Apple Ads campaign, Haly passes Apple’s AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so we can see which campaign led to a subscription. Apple provides this attribution without an advertising identifier or cross-app tracking. Haly does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not ask for tracking permission and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Analytics and advertising

Haly contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs and no separate analytics SDK. RevenueCat’s dashboard gives us aggregated purchase statistics. We do not sell your data.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. Please do not send health records unless they are needed to solve your issue. You can delete all of Haly’s local data in the App under Data & backup, or by deleting the App. You can request access, correction or deletion of personal data we or RevenueCat hold by contacting support; an anonymous RevenueCat identifier or App Store order reference may be needed to locate purchase records. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Children

Haly is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for adults managing their own records.

Changes

This page is updated when Haly’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
