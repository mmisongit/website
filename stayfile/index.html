Stayfile support

Every day in the right place: Stayfile records which country and US state you are in and counts your days for Schengen 90/180, 183-day rules and the US substantial presence test. No account; your locations stay on your iPhone.

Getting started Purchases Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Pick the limits you want to watch: Schengen 90/180, the UK tax year, the US presence test, a country’s 183 days or a US state.
  2. Turn on automatic tracking and allow location “Always”. Stayfile uses the iPhone’s low-power location changes, not continuous GPS.
  3. Add trips from before you started, or planned trips, with the plus button.
  4. Tap any number to see the days behind it. Open a day to see how it was recorded and to correct it if needed.
  5. Export a PDF and CSV record for any period from the Export tab.

Stayfile counts days. It is not tax or legal advice and does not decide whether you are resident anywhere; that also depends on your home, family, work and tax treaties.

Purchases

Stayfile Pro

The free plan lets you enter days and trips by hand and use every limit and the Schengen planner. Stayfile Pro adds automatic recording, warnings before a limit and PDF/CSV export. It is available as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers, as a monthly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Stayfile → Settings → Restore Purchases. To cancel a subscription, open your Apple Account settings → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Without Pro, automatic recording pauses; every recorded day stays.

Data & privacy

Stayfile has no account and no server. Locations, days, trips and corrections stay on your iPhone; the country and state are worked out on the device without a network. Reports and backups leave the app only when you share or save them yourself.

Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases. Stayfile contains no analytics, tracking or ads. Read the privacy policy and Apple standard terms of use.

Help & troubleshooting

Days are missing or marked “not confirmed”

  • In the iOS Settings app, set Location for Stayfile to “Always” and turn on Precise Location.
  • Keep Background App Refresh on for Stayfile.
  • If the phone stays in one place, Stayfile carries the day over; after three days without any location it asks you to confirm. Open the app or tap “Confirm” on the day.

A day near a border shows the wrong country

  • Open the day and tap “Correct this day”. Your correction is listed in every export next to the original record.

Moving to a new iPhone

  • Use Settings → Export Backup and save the file in the Files app; restore it on the new iPhone with Restore Backup. Stayfile data is also part of your iPhone backup.
