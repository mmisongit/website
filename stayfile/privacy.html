Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Stayfile is provided by Mohamed Makled (“we”). For privacy questions, contact support@mmcapitals.com.

Your locations stay on your device

With your permission, Stayfile uses Location Services to record where your device is: visits and significant location changes reported by iOS, a location when you open the App, and occasionally one during a background refresh. From each location the App works out the country and, in the United States, the state, using boundary data stored in the App, without a network request. The locations, the resulting days, trips you enter, corrections, your limits and the name you enter for reports are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. Stayfile has no account, no cloud sync and no server of ours, and we never receive your locations.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, iOS may include Stayfile’s local data in that backup. Such backups are handled by Apple or by your computer under their own terms.

You can withdraw location permission at any time in the iOS Settings app, or turn off automatic tracking in Stayfile; recorded days stay until you delete the App.

Notifications

Warnings before a limit and reminders to confirm your location are local notifications created on your device. No push service is used.

Reports and backups you share

PDF reports, CSV files and backup files are created on your device. They leave the App only when you share or save them through the iOS share sheet or the Files app, to the recipients and places you choose. The optional capture CSV contains coordinates, rounded to about 1 km unless you choose exact coordinates.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for Stayfile Pro. Stayfile uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain the Stayfile Pro entitlement and restore purchases. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated App User ID, purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase status and dates, and app, device and store metadata. No name, email, payment-card details or location data are supplied by Stayfile to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase records are retained as needed to provide and restore your purchase and to meet legal obligations. RevenueCat may process data outside your country under its published data-processing terms.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

Stayfile contains no analytics SDK. It does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, does not use the advertising identifier and shows no ads. We do not sell your data.

Not advice

Stayfile counts days. It does not provide tax, legal or immigration advice and does not decide residency or visa status.

Support and your choices

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer you. You can delete all of Stayfile’s local data by deleting the App. You can request access to or deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat by contacting support; a purchase date or App Store order reference helps us locate them. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to correction, restriction, objection, portability and to complain to a data-protection authority.

Children

Stayfile is a tool for adults managing travel and tax-residency days and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This page is updated when Stayfile’s data processing changes. The latest date appears above.
