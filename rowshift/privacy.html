Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rowshift: Collection Sort (the “App”), a Shopify app provided by Mohamed Makled (the “Provider”, “we”), handles information when a merchant installs it on a Shopify store. The Provider is responsible for this processing and can be reached at support@mmcapitals.com.

At a glance: Rowshift reorders products in your collections based on stock. It reads products, collections, inventory and locations. It never requests orders or customer data, so it holds no information about your shoppers. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking.

What the App accesses

When you install Rowshift, Shopify grants it these permissions: read and write products (to read your collections and change the product order), read inventory and read locations (to know which sizes are in stock at the locations that sell online). The App does not request access to orders, customers, checkouts or payments.

What we store

  • Your shop: the shop’s myshopify domain and the access token Shopify issues for the App, so the App can sort in the background.
  • Your settings: which collections are switched on, the broken-size threshold, pinned products, schedule, and your plan.
  • Collection data: collection and product IDs, the product order before the first sort (so you can restore it with one click), and which inventory items belong to which products.
  • Activity log: when each sort or rollback ran, how many products moved, how long it took and whether it failed.

We do not store product descriptions, prices or images beyond what is needed to show the preview while you use the App.

Customer data

Rowshift does not access, receive or store personal data about your customers. Shopify’s mandatory privacy webhooks for customer data requests and customer erasure are answered without any data, because there is none.

Store staff

The App uses Shopify’s session handling to confirm that a request comes from your store’s admin. It does not keep staff names or email addresses.

Where data is processed

The App runs on servers operated by Fly.io in Frankfurt, Germany, with its database in the same region. Shopify processes data as the platform the App is built on. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Billing

Plans and trials are handled by Shopify. The App only reads which plan your store has; it never sees payment details.

Retention and deletion

When you uninstall Rowshift, the access token is deleted at once and all sorting stops. Shopify then sends a shop erasure request 48 hours later, and we delete all remaining data for your store: settings, saved product orders and logs. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email support@mmcapitals.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to answer you.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The current version and its date are always posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mmcapitals.com.
