Rowshift support

Rowshift: Collection Sort is a Shopify app that keeps sold-out products and products with missing sizes off the first rows of your collections. It changes only the product order, so it works with every theme and needs no theme code.

Getting started Plans Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Install Rowshift from the Shopify App Store and pick a plan. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.
  2. Confirm which locations count as stock. By default, every location that fulfils online orders counts.
  3. Open Collections, pick a collection and look at the Before/After preview. Nothing changes in your store until you click Save and sort now.
  4. Turn on Auto-sort to keep the collection in order every day, or every hour on Pro.

How it sorts

Each product lands in one of three groups: in stock first, then broken sizes, then sold out. A product has broken sizes when fewer than the share of sizes you choose (50% by default) are in stock. Within each group, the order you had stays the same unless you pick another secondary sort.

Pre-orders, untracked and pinned products

Products that keep selling when out of stock, and products whose inventory is not tracked, count as in stock. Pinned products always stay at the top in the order you set; you can choose to move them down when they sell out.

Automated collections

Shopify only allows a custom order in manual sort mode. The first sort switches an automated collection (for example “Best selling”) to manual order and saves the old setting so you can restore it.

Undo a sort

Before the first sort, Rowshift saves the collection’s order. Open the collection and click Restore original order to put the products back and turn auto-sort off. For a collection that was automated, its original sort setting comes back.

Plans

  • Starter, $19/month: up to 10 active collections, daily auto-sort, pinning, preview and rollback.
  • Growth, $39/month: unlimited collections, sorting within 5 minutes of a stock change, all secondary sorts and tag boost.
  • Pro, $69/month: hourly schedule, choosing exactly which locations count, collections with more than 5,000 products.

Billing runs through Shopify and appears on your Shopify invoice. You can change or cancel your plan in the app under Plans.

Data & privacy

Rowshift reads products, collections, inventory and locations. It never requests orders or customer data. When you uninstall, sorting stops at once and your collections keep their last order. All data for your store is deleted 48 hours later. Read the privacy policy.

Help & troubleshooting

A sold-out product is still near the top

  • Check whether the product is pinned.
  • Check whether it sells as a pre-order or has untracked inventory; both count as in stock.
  • Check Settings: stock at locations that do not fulfil online orders does not count.

The order did not change after a stock change

  • Sorting after stock changes needs Growth or Pro and the option Sort when inventory changes. It runs 5 minutes after the last change, so a burst of changes leads to a single sort.
  • The Activity page lists every sort with its result.
