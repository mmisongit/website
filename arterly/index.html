Arterly support

A simple, private logbook for your blood pressure. Your readings stay on your iPhone and in Apple Health.

Getting started Purchases Data & privacy Help

Getting started

  1. Measure with your own blood pressure monitor. A phone cannot measure blood pressure.
  2. Open Arterly, tap Add reading and enter the top number, the bottom number and, if you like, your pulse and a note.
  3. Arterly places each reading in its zone and keeps the history. You can edit or delete any reading later.

Zones follow the ACC/AHA categories for adults. Readings taken at the same times each day are the most useful; you can set a morning and an evening reminder in Settings.

Purchases

Arterly Pro

Logging, the full history, the 7-day trend, Apple Health sync, reminders and the spreadsheet export (CSV) are free. Arterly Pro adds the doctor report as a PDF and trends over 30 and 90 days. It is available as a yearly subscription, with a free trial where offered, or as a weekly subscription. The App Store shows your local price before you confirm.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Use the same Apple Account as for your purchase. Open Arterly → Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel, open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

Data & privacy

Where your readings are stored

Arterly has no account. Readings are stored on your iPhone. If you turn on Sync with Apple Health, Arterly reads blood pressure readings from Apple Health and saves the ones you enter there. iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may also include Arterly’s data.

Your readings are not sent to us or to any third party. Apple handles purchases. Read the privacy policy. Arterly is a logbook, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice.

Help & troubleshooting

Readings from my monitor do not appear

  • Check that your monitor’s app saves readings to Apple Health.
  • In Arterly, open Settings and turn on Sync with Apple Health.
  • In the iOS Settings app, open Health → Data Access & Devices → Arterly and allow blood pressure.

Reminders do not arrive

  • In Arterly, open Settings → Reminders and turn on Morning or Evening.
  • In the iOS Settings app, check Notifications → Arterly and your Focus modes.

Contact support

Need help? Email support@mmcapitals.com with your iOS version, Arterly version and a description of the issue. Please avoid sending health records.
