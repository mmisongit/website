Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Arterly: Blood Pressure Log app (the “App”), provided by Mohamed Makled (the “Provider”, “we”), handles information. The Provider is responsible for processing in connection with the App and can be reached at support@mmcapitals.com.

At a glance: Your blood pressure readings stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on the sync, in Apple Health. Arterly has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Provider does not receive your readings. Apple processes purchases.

Your readings

Blood pressure values, pulse, notes, the time of each reading and your reminder times are stored locally on your iPhone. The Provider does not receive, store or have access to this data. Your readings are included in your device backup under your Apple Account.

Apple Health

If you turn on Sync with Apple Health, the App reads blood pressure readings from Apple Health, so that readings saved by your monitor’s app appear in your history, and writes the readings you enter in the App to Apple Health. The App asks for access to blood pressure only. Edits and deletions in the App update or remove the matching readings it saved. Data read from or written to Apple Health stays on your device, is not sent to the Provider or any third party, and is not used for advertising or marketing. You can turn off the sync in the App’s Settings and revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Reports and exports

The doctor report (PDF) and the spreadsheet export (CSV) are created on your device. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, for example by email or by printing.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service is used.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on your device. We do not receive your payment-card information, and the App does not send purchase data to any analytics or advertising service. Apple may provide us with aggregated sales reports that do not identify you. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account or registration.
  • No analytics of how you use the App and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to answer you. Please do not send health records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Your readings stay on your device, so you control them directly: you can edit or delete readings in the App at any time. Deleting the App removes the data it stored on your device. Readings saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app. For questions, email support@mmcapitals.com.

Not a medical device

Arterly is a logbook. It does not measure blood pressure and does not replace advice from your doctor.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The current version and its date are always posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mmcapitals.com.
